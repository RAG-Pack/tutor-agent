# Example answers

**Student:** What was the headlight thing Luis talked about?

**Tutor:** Luis compared a transformer to a car with a tiny headlight and a huge
rear-view mirror: it sees the whole conversation behind it, but only one word ahead,
because it writes one word at a time. That's why a model reads (spam detection: lots in,
one word out) better than it writes. Luis explains it in the Session 1 recording from
**24:03** to about 28:00, roughly 4 minutes:
[watch from 24:03](https://www.ragpack.ai/products/introduction-to-agents/categories/2160909364/posts/2201045339?video_start_at=1443).
Does the "one word ahead" part make sense?
